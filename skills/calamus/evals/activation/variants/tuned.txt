Project memory: append-only journals of engineering decisions (docs/dev_journal.md) and product insights (docs/product_insights.md). Use whenever the user or session decides, pivots, rejects an option, fixes a non-obvious bug, hits a constraint, or verifies a milestone; whenever user pain, UX friction, positioning, roadmap trade-offs, or growth signals come up; and whenever someone asks why something was decided or what was learned earlier. Also use to resolve CALAMUS-PENDING markers. Not for changelogs, release notes, commit messages, code comments, or application logging.
